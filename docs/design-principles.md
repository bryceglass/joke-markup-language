# Design Principles

1. **Comedy first:** preserve bits, jokes, beats, setups, punches, tags and callbacks as semantic constructs.
2. **Semantic by default:** no requirement to specify pitch or milliseconds.
3. **Precision on demand:** permit explicit delivery and synchronization.
4. **Performer independent:** no hard dependency on TTS, animation, pumpkins, or any runtime.
5. **Interoperable:** align with established speech, emotion and timing standards where useful.
6. **Graceful degradation:** renderers can ignore unsupported modalities without altering dialogue.
7. **Human-readable, machine-validatable:** XML interchange first; friendlier authoring may follow.
8. **Dialogue integrity:** intent annotations do not authorize rewriting.
9. **Open and portable:** multiple independent consumers should be possible.
