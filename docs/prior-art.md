# Prior Art and Interoperability

This inventory is preliminary; it does not assert that JML is novel or unique.

- [W3C SSML 1.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/speech-synthesis11/): pitch, rate, volume, pauses, pronunciation. Study export compatibility.
- [W3C EmotionML](https://www.w3.org/TR/emotionml/): emotion categories, dimensions and intensity. Study attitude semantics.
- [W3C SMIL](https://www.w3.org/TR/SMIL3/): synchronized multimedia and event timing. Study scheduling semantics.
- [Fountain](https://fountain.io/): human-friendly screenplay syntax. Study authoring experience.
- [Speech Markdown](https://github.com/speechmarkdown/speechmarkdown-js): speech authoring and SSML conversion. Study lightweight notation.

JML focuses on **comedy-specific semantic structure linked to optional executable performance direction**. Interoperability adapters must disclose unsupported features and information loss; JML does not claim conformance to these standards.
